Our story

A table that feels like home.

At Sabor Caseiro, we believe the best meals are never only about what is on the plate. They are about the people around the table, the smell from the kitchen and the feeling that you can stay for one more conversation.

Our menu brings together Portuguese comfort food and Mediterranean influences, using seasonal produce and simple techniques to let every ingredient speak for itself.
